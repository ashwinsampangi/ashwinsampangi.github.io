Excel template · Nov 2026

An AI agent row for the delegation-of-authority matrix

An AI agent should hold no approval authority of its own. It applies rules that a named person has signed.

This workbook adds the agent row to your matrix and lists each rule with its owner, signing date, item and period limits (value and count, per supplier and in total) and review date. It flags any rule that is incomplete, exceeds its owner's own limit, or is marked as covering an action no rule may approve, such as a new payee or a bank-detail change.

A period tab then checks what the agent actually cleared against those limits, including anything cleared under a rule that had lapsed.
